Models – your data structure (what the database stores)

Views – your logic (what happens when a request comes in)

URLs – your routing (which view runs for which page)

Templates – your HTML (what the user sees)

Forms – your validation (clean + check user input)

Settings – your global config (database, apps, static files, login rules)

Middleware – automatic filters (auth, sessions, security)

Admin – Django’s built-in backend to manage data

Static files – CSS, JS, images

Apps – organised modules (complaints, accounts, etc.)
